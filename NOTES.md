First release of Imprint, a watermark app for Android.

- Put your mark on photos, PDFs and videos, from one library
- An editor with words, logos, 14 shapes, a signature and QR codes, and 120 designs to start from
- 1,824 typefaces: 204 built in, the rest fetched on first use, plus your own font files
- Single, tiled and cross placements, batches of photos with a review strip, presets and brand kits
- Live stamps for the date, time, file name, a running number, and where a photo was taken
- PDFs keep their real, searchable text; videos keep their sound and can carry a moving mark
- A backup file for everything you have made
- No adverts, no accounts, no tracking. Your photos never leave your phone

**Good to know:** removing Imprint deletes your library, so save a backup file from Settings first. Your original photos are never changed; copies go to a folder called Imprint. The extras are switched off to begin with, under Settings, More tools. This first version was checked with automated tests on a computer and has not yet been tried across a range of phones. See https://customgpter.github.io/imprint/#first
