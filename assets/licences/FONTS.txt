TYPEFACES IN IMPRINT
====================

Every typeface below comes from Google Fonts and is used under the licence named beside it.
The full licence texts are in this folder: OFL-1.1.txt, Apache-2.0.txt and UFL-1.0.txt.

Built in: the regular weight of each of these families is inside the app. Those marked
(Latin subset) hold the Latin and extended Latin letters only, with nothing else changed;
the rest are the files exactly as Google Fonts publishes them.

Listed: for every other family the app holds only a small file with the letters of the
family's own name, as published by Google Fonts, so that the name can be shown in its own
style. Those marked (name outlines only) have been cut down to just those outlines. The
family itself is fetched from Google Fonts, unaltered, the first time it is chosen.
