Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. This policy covers the Imprint app for Android (package uk.jj.imprint) and this website. Imprint is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Imprint has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics in the app.
  • Your photos, PDFs and videos are read and watermarked on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • The app goes online for two things: to fetch a typeface from Google Fonts the first time you choose one that is not built in, and to look up an address from a photo’s coordinates if you use the Address stamp. Both are described below.
  • Imprint does not use your camera, your microphone, your contacts or the phone’s own location.

What stays on your phone

Imprint keeps the following in its private storage:

  • The watermarks you have made, with the logos, signatures and QR codes inside them.
  • Your presets and brand kits.
  • Typefaces you have fetched or added.
  • Your settings, including a project name, an author name and a copyright line if you have typed them in.
  • When each watermark was last used and how often, so that Home can show your recent ones.

The watermarked copies you save are ordinary files in the phone’s Pictures, Documents and Movies folders, each in a folder called Imprint. They stay there if you uninstall the app. Imprint keeps no list of the photos, PDFs or videos you have watermarked. The app is set so that Android does not include its data in the phone’s backup to your Google account; the only backup is the file you save yourself.

Services the app contacts

Imprint has no server of its own. Your phone connects directly to the services below. Each sees your internet address and the technical details any web server sees, and handles them under its own privacy policy. None of them is sent your photos, your watermarks or your name.

Imprint does not look for new versions by itself. With Location switched off, which is how the app starts, the address lookup is never used.

What goes into your copies

A photo carries details inside its file: the date, the camera, and sometimes the place. Because a watermarked copy is usually made to be shared, it is worth knowing what Imprint puts in it.

  • With Keep photo details on, which is how the app starts, the copy carries the original’s date and camera details. Switch it off to leave them out.
  • The place a photo was taken is carried into the copy only when Location is switched on. With it off, Android has already removed the place before Imprint sees the photo.
  • With Write my name into the file on, the author and copyright line you typed are written into each copy.
  • A live stamp prints what it says onto the picture itself: a date, a file name, coordinates or an address become part of the image and cannot be taken out of it afterwards.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • A watermark, preset or brand kit is deleted from its own menu in the Library.
  • Saved copies are ordinary files. Delete them with your gallery or file manager.
  • Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling Imprint deletes everything it holds: watermarks, presets, brand kits, typefaces and settings. Saved copies stay.
  • A backup file you saved is wherever you put it. It holds your watermarks, presets, brand kits, logos, signatures, added typefaces and settings. Delete it there when you no longer want it.
  • Nothing is held by the developer, so there is nothing further to delete.

Your rights

UK and EU data protection law gives you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you, including the right to see it, correct it and have it deleted.

The developer of Imprint holds no personal data about you: there is no account, no server and no log of who uses the app. What Imprint stores is on your own phone, where you control it directly using the steps above. For data held by one of the services listed, contact that service. If you need help, use the contact link below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

What Imprint stores is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. A backup file is not encrypted: anyone who has the file can open it and see your watermarks, logos and signature, so keep it as carefully as you would the signature itself. Connections to Google Fonts are encrypted.

No software is free of faults. Imprint’s source code is public so that anyone can check what it does.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the app file is downloaded from GitHub Releases. GitHub, as the host, receives the technical details any web server sees, such as your IP address and browser type, and handles them under the GitHub privacy statement. The developer does not receive them.

Children

Imprint is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone. A parent or guardian should decide whether a child uses it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published here.

Contact

For a question about privacy or your data, open an issue on GitHub. Please do not put private details in an issue; it is public.
