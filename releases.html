What was added, improved and fixed in each version of Imprint, newest first. The current version is 1.0.

Version 1.0

First release

LatestReleased 19 November 2026

Added

  • Watermarks on photos, PDFs and videos, from one library.
  • An editor with words, logos and 14 shapes; outline, shadow, glow, a box behind the words and words bent round a curve; undo and redo; logo recolouring and background removal.
  • 120 starter designs in eight groups, all changeable.
  • 1,824 typefaces: 204 built in, the rest fetched from Google Fonts on first use, with recent and starred lists, and your own TTF and OTF files.
  • Three placements: Single (nine positions or dragged by hand), Tiled (angle, spacing and stagger) and Cross. Size, strength, a colour filter, and automatic light or dark.
  • Hold the picture to compare with and without the mark.
  • Batches of photos with a review strip, and This photo only for moving the mark on one photo.
  • A Home screen with your last used watermark, favourites and recents; a Library with search, favourites, last used and most used.
  • Presets that remember a watermark and how it sits. Brand kits with their own colours, typefaces and logo.
  • Live stamps: date, time, year, file name, running number, project, and with Location on, coordinates and address.
  • Signature drawn with a finger, and QR codes from any text or web address.
  • PDF: the pages keep their real text. Page ranges, over the page or behind the words, and optional headers, footers and page numbers.
  • Video: MP4 with the sound kept. Optional timing, fade, and a mark that bounces around or drifts across the frame.
  • Saving as JPG, PNG or WebP with quality, a smaller size, and file names from a pattern. Photo details kept or left out. Your name and a copyright line written into the file.
  • A backup file for watermarks, presets, brand kits, logos, signatures, added fonts and settings, which can be added to what is on a phone or replace it.
  • Dark, light and same-as-phone themes.

Known limits

  • A large batch or a long video can be stopped by Android if Imprint is swiped away while it is saving.
  • One PDF and one video at a time. Password-locked PDFs are refused.
  • On a PDF the watermark is added as a picture; the page’s own text is untouched.
  • A moving video mark applies to the Single placement.
  • SVG logos are not accepted.
  • Imprint does not look for new versions by itself. Settings, About has a link to this site.

Installing and updating

  • When you download, Chrome may warn that the file might be harmful. It shows this warning for app files that do not come from Google Play. Choose Download anyway.
  • Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow your browser or file manager to install apps. Allow it, then tap Install.
  • Google Play Protect may say it does not recognise the app, or block it, because Imprint does not come from Google Play. Choose More details, then Install anyway.
  • Open Imprint. It asks for no permissions to begin with. Tap Templates to pick a design, or Blank to start with an empty canvas.
  • The extras, such as live stamps, location, signature and QR code, are under Settings › More tools.
  • To update, download the latest version and install it over the one you have. Your library and settings are kept.
  • Before you uninstall Imprint or change phone, save a backup file from Settings › Backup. Uninstalling deletes the library.
  • Every version is signed with the same key, so Android accepts an update only if it comes from the same developer.
